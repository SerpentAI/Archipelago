# Day of the Tentacle

## Randomizer Summary

Day of the Tentacle is already a game that requires you to switch between three characters in three different time periods, sending items through the Chron-O-Johns. This randomizer embraces it. The items, which you should have found in the past, present, and future levels are mixed randomly across the multiworld, and your own items may appear even far earlier than the game allows you to get them normally. The time eras become available in a random sequence, and the puzzles may be solved in any order your items allow.

Bernard, Hoagie and Laverne are items as well. You start as one of them and you will have to find the other two first to be able to control them or to send some items through the Chron-O-Johns. The received items become immediately available for you to use in the inventory of your current character, and Laverne starts directly in the kennel.

**Locations**
- Picking Up Items
- Solving Puzzles
- Visiting Rooms (Optional)
- Triggering Unique Voice Lines (Optional)

**Items**
- Inventory Items
- Characters
- Swiss Deposits


## Randomizer Features
- 2 Goals to choose from:
  - `Stop Purple Tentacle`: Collect enough Swiss Deposits for Bernard to order the diamond, then power the Chron-O-Johns and stop Purple Tentacle
  - `Swiss Bank Heist`: Collect enough Swiss Deposits and the Swiss Bankbook, then have Bernard walk out of the motel's front door with the bankbook, abandoning his friends
- Ability to include Room Visit locations (+49 locations)
- Ability to include a subset of Voice Lines as locations (+39 locations)
- Ability to select / randomize your Starting Character (and therefore era)
- Custom client UI that displays important information and updates in real-time
- No manual mod installation required! The Archipelago client hooks into the game at runtime. Nothing else to download, install, or configure.
- Feature-complete and tested


## Differences from the Original Game
- Bernard can't leave the basement lab until the intro is over. Afterwards, the starting items are taken away and you switch to your starting character.
- Laverne doesn't need rescuing: she starts in the kennel, and Washington's tree-chopping cutscene is skipped.
- The diamond can only be ordered once the required Swiss Deposits are in, and never in `Swiss Bank Heist`.
- Laverne can lower the flag while disguised.
- The contract can be stamped, signed and mailed at any time.
- Any inventory item can be sent to another character through the Chron-O-John. Some items can only be sent with the Give command.


## Tracking

**Universal Tracker**

The APWorld is fully compatible with Universal Tracker. It is a YAML-free implementation (i.e. you don't need to have the YAML used to generate in your Players directory). If Universal Tracker is installed, the client will automatically embed the Tracker tab, and the `Day of the Tentacle` tab will list the in-logic locations for the era of the character you are playing.
